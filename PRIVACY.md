# Privacy Policy

mail-reminder is a personal-use script that reads the owner's own Gmail
(read-only) to produce a short summary of important emails.

- It accesses only the owner's own Gmail account.
- Email content is processed in memory and is not stored, sold or shared.
- Summaries are sent only to the owner's own WhatsApp number.
- No data is collected from any other users.
